Aart (Arie) van Zanten born in Naarden on September 28, 1801, baptised in Naarden on September 30, 1801 (the "*/~" abbreviation encapsulates this), died in Naarden May 1, 1848, worked as a mason's assistant (metselaarsknecht) between 1823 and 1829. Worked as a mason (metselaar) between 1834 and 1840 and a bricklayer / laborer in 1848. Married on July 20, 1823 to Anna Antonia (Antje) Kappers who was born in Winterswijk on February 1 1800 and Baptised in Winterswijk on February 19, 1800 (again "*/~" captures that). She died in Naarden on Augues 23, 1856. She worked as a maid and was the daughter of ("dv") Hendrik Willem Kappers, who was a cooper, and Amelia Poppink. Aart van Zanten and Anna Antonia Kappers children are listed under VI.1 (that's what the "VI.1" at the end of that statement means). In 1829 Aart lived at St. Annastraat 210 in Naarden. Between 1839 and 1842 he lived at Turfpoortstraat 256 in Naarden. In 1848 he lived at Schipperstraat 434 in Naarden. His wife was listed at Bussemmerstraat 146a in Naarden (not sure of the year here or any other context but that's what it says).
